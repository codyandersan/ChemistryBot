ChemBot
Hi, I'm ChemBot. I can explain NCERT chemistry concepts (Classes 9 to 12), and for numericals I give hints and point out mistakes rather than just handing over the final answer. Ask me anything you're stuck on.

About ChemBot

This project is built as an exhibition of how AI can be integrated with class 12 chemistry study.

Why this over ChatGPT or Gemini directly?

  • Hints, not handouts. For numerical problems, ChemBot points you toward the right concept and flags where you've gone wrong, instead of just giving you the final number to copy.
  • Scoped to your syllabus. It only draws from the NCERT chemistry syllabus across Classes 9–12, so answers stay exam-relevant instead of wandering into unrelated territory a general-purpose chatbot might bring in.
  • One job, no clutter. No sign-up, no unrelated features — just a focused space for chemistry doubts.
  • Built by a student, for students. Made specifically around how CBSE students actually get stuck, not as a general-purpose assistant.
